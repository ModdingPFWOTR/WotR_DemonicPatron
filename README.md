# Demonic Patron

A rogue archetype for Pathfinder: Wrath of the Righteous.

Through a secret pact with a demon, the rogue gains elemental power. A Demonic Patron is an Eldritch Scoundrel who gives up danger sense and finesse training. Weapon Finesse, spellcasting, and the rest of the scoundrel's training remain.

Create a new rogue and choose **Demonic Patron**. At 3rd level, choose a demon. That patron has its own progression, separate from the rogue feature list.

## Patrons

The elemental damage is a toggle, on by default. It applies to every weapon you wield.

The save DC for a patron strike is **10 + half your rogue level + your Intelligence modifier**.

| Patron | 3rd level | 11th level | 16th level |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| **Flauros**, demon of flame | 1d6 fire | **Fiery Strike.** Every 3 successful sneak attacks, the target and each enemy within 10 feet take 2d6 fire damage. The count carries across turns in the same combat. | **Profane Fire.** Weapon damage becomes 2d6 fire, and Fiery Strike deals 4d6. |
| **Sithhud**, demon of frost | 1d6 cold | **Freezing Strike.** Every 2 successful sneak attacks in the same turn, the target makes a Reflex save or is staggered for 1 round. | **Frost Rebuke.** When you enter a battle, you gain Vengeful Comets at your highest caster level, without spending a spell slot. |
| **Pazuzu**, demon of lightning | 1d6 electricity | **Static Strike.** Every 2 successful sneak attacks in the same turn, the target makes a Will save or is dazed for 1 round. | **Static Discharge.** The first time each turn you hit a creature that is already dazed, that creature is struck by Chain Lightning at your highest caster level, without spending a spell slot. |
| **Aldinach**, demon of poison | 1d6 acid | **Sickening Strike.** Every 2 successful sneak attacks in the same turn, the target makes a Fortitude save or is sickened for 1d3 rounds. | **Sickened Contagion.** When you kill a sickened enemy, the corpse bursts. Each enemy within 10 feet takes 2d6 acid damage and is sickened for 2 rounds. |

Aldinach's venom deals acid damage. The game has no separate poison damage type. The poison itself is the sickened effect.

A buff with the strike's name shows how many sneak attacks you have counted. A miss does not reset the count. Freezing Strike, Static Strike, and Sickening Strike reset at the start of your next turn. Fiery Strike keeps its count until it bursts or combat ends. Every counter ends when combat ends.

## Removed features

- Danger Sense at 3rd, 6th, 9th, 12th, 15th, and 18th level
- Finesse Training at 3rd, 11th, and 19th level

## Requirements

- Pathfinder: Wrath of the Righteous 2.7.0
- Unity Mod Manager 0.23.0 or newer

Frost Rebuke uses Vengeful Comets, which is part of the game's later spell content.
